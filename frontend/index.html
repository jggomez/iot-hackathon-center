Thermal Stress Guide

In closed events with many people, thermal stress from overcrowding occurs. The human body acts as a heat radiator and a constant humidifier. Humidity is critical: if the air is saturated, sweat does not evaporate, and the thermal sensation skyrockets, risking heat stroke or fainting.

Alert Level Temp Range Hum Range Diagnosis System Action
🟢 Normal < 26°C 40% - 60% Optimal comfort. Controlled capacity and adequate ventilation. All in order. LED Off. Normal telemetry.
🟡 Warning 26°C - 29.9°C 61% - 70% Nearly full / Insufficient ventilation. Air starts to feel heavy. Preventive alert via MQTT. Slow LED blinking.
🔴 Critical Danger ≥ 30°C > 70% Overcrowding / Risk of heat stroke. Exceeds safety standards. Red Alert. Fixed LED on. Urgent MQTT alarm.

AI Analysis Legend

Class 0: DEEP_FOCUS

Workshop / Concentration Mode

What to record: People typing actively on laptops, low murmurs, fans, air conditioning, sliding chairs, or notebook pages.

Acoustic signature: Continuous, flat sound, without sudden volume spikes.

Class 1: HIGH_ENGAGEMENT

Talk / Keynote Mode

What to record: Constant voice speaking via microphone, followed by bursts of applause, collective laughter, or exclamations.

Acoustic signature: Alternation between a dominant voice frequency and abrupt energy peaks (applause).

Class 2: ROOM_EMPTY

Empty or Break Mode

What to record: Total silence or minimal ambient noise without human presence.

Acoustic signature: Base noise level, absence of voice patterns or rhythmic mechanical activity.
